# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, product categories, discounts, and sales trends.

The main dataset contains 9,994 order-level records and 23 fields, covering orders from 2014 to 2017.

---

## Objectives

1. Measure overall sales and profitability.
2. Identify high-performing categories, sub-categories, regions, and customer segments.
3. Analyze sales and profit trends over time.
4. Understand the effect of discount levels on profitability.
5. Compare average sales and average profit across business dimensions.
6. Analyze the relationship between Sales and Profit.
7. Build a concise business-focused view of sales performance and profitability.

## Process

### 1. Data Preparation

- Reviewed the Orders, Returns, and People tables.
- Checked order dates, sales, quantity, discount, profit, category, region, segment, and sub-category fields.
- Used Excel data-cleaning techniques such as trimming, formatting, and validation.

### 2. Exploratory Analysis

- Calculated total sales, total profit, average sales, and average profit.
- Compared performance by Category, Sub-Category, Region, and Segment.
- Analyzed monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Examined discount levels across business categories.
